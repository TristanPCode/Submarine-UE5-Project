CURRENT GAMEPLAY / ARCHITECTURE
===============================

Core Pawn:
ASubmarinePawn

* Uses custom USubmarinePhysicsComponent
* 6DOF movement
* Pitch interpolation already exists
* Uses ITrackableSubmarine
* Has radar + torpedo systems
* Uses different sensitivity profiles for keyboard/gamepad

Current movement issue:

* Current pitch affects vertical motion too directly
* Near surface behavior feels unrealistic
* Submarine can “fly” upward too easily

Future goal:

* Separate:
  CommandPitch
  DesiredPitch
  ActualPitch
* Real inertia/heaviness
* Buoyancy-based vertical movement

For now the Physics work well and are consistent with underwater Physics. There's no real water for now.
Even in the Physics component, it just checks if we are above/under a certain height in our world.
The goal is to remove that and have a clean water visualisation and physics application.

====================================================
IMPORTANT DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
=============================

1. WATER HEIGHT AND WATER AGITATION ARE SEPARATE SYSTEMS

We do NOT want a single “wave height”.

We want:

* average water height
* agitation intensity
* turbulence
  all separated.

The current idea:
RGBA texture channels:

R = average water height
G = agitation intensity
B = turbidity / underwater visibility
A = reserved for future use

Question:
Is this the best architecture?
Or should some data be generated procedurally instead?

====================================================
2) REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM
========================

We want:

* different sea regions
* different wave intensity
* different average water heights
* different fog/turbidity/color
* future weather compatibility
